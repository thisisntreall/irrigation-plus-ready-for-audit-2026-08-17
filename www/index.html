Services

What we actually do in the yard

New system install

Walk the lot, map zones, set heads for coverage — not a one-size grid. Quote before any trench work.

Expansion & add-on zones

New beds, a side yard that never got water, or a rebuilt lawn after construction.

Repair & diagnosis

Broken heads, low pressure, wet spots, dry corners. Find it, fix it, leave the clock right.

Controller programming

Neighbors specifically mention this. Seasonal schedules that match Alabama heat, not factory defaults.

Seasonal startup

Spring turn-on, zone check, and a run-through before the first hot week hits.

Mid-season tune

Heads knocked by mowers, leaks at fittings, valves that won’t close. Short visit, not a project.

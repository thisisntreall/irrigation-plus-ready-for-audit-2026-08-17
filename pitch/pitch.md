## Objections

- **“I have Facebook.”** Facebook is where you post. Google is where strangers search at 7am with a geyser in the yard. The site is the phone number they can tap.
- **“I don’t need a website.”** You already had one (GoDaddy) and it died. That’s worse than none — it looks closed.
- **“Too much money.”** Basic package. No monthly if they hate subscriptions.
- **“I’m slammed in season.”** That’s why the site only has one action: call you. We don’t add booking software.
- **“I don’t want my home address online.”** Good — the draft is written as a service area. We confirm the pin with you.

## What not to say

- Don’t claim I-CAR, licenses, or “#1 in Pell City.”  
- Don’t quote a star rating. One Nextdoor rec is a quote, not 5.0.  
- Don’t promise same-day unless he says so.  
- Don’t pick a single street address until he chooses.
